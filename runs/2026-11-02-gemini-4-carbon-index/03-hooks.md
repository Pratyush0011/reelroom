# Hooks: Gemini 4 Carbon is #1 on Artificial Analysis's Index v5

Mode: news. Only `verified` / `verified_with_wording` claims used, in safe_text wording.
Not used, per the fact-checker: "launched" (we say announced / ranked), "9-point jump in two weeks", "most accurate on AA-Omniscience", "15 of 17 benchmark rows". No hook implies the public can use Carbon today.

## Angles
A1: Anyone who picks an AI by "who's #1". The crown is 59 vs 58, Carbon (high) vs Claude Opus 5.5 (max), on an index that came out the day before Carbon took #1, and a second independent tester ranks it #2. So "#1" is a weak reason to choose or pay for a chatbot.
A2: Everyday users who hear "Google's new best AI" and expect to try it. They can't. Google announced it and is giving it first to a small group of cyber defenders, with no date for developers or consumers.
A3: Developers and businesses budgeting for AI. Artificial Analysis says it does the index tasks for less than Opus 5.5 ($7.76 vs $10.04 per task), but the $2/$10 intro price goes up to $4/$20 with no end date, it trails Claude Code on AA's coding-agent test, and it makes things up more often than its predecessor.

## Notes for the critic and scriptwriter
- Top picks: H5 (cleanest say-back, carries the whole story), H1 (one number, one idea, instantly clear), H3 (the honest twist in four words). Dark horse: H4, which is the sharpest insider detail but depends on viewers getting "high" vs "medium" mode.
- H1/H4/H10/H11: the 59 is Carbon (high) only. Carbon (medium) is also 58. If the script says "as of today", use "as of 2 Nov" (C17).
- H2: never attach a time span to 50 → 59 (no "two weeks"). Don't mention Argon's old 53, which is on the older index.
- H3: the Vals leader is Claude Sonnet 5.5, not Opus. Say "about 70%" or use the rank only, never 70.36%.
- H7/H8: the cyber scores must be voiced as "Google reports", tested without guardrails, not independently checked (C15). H8 promises a "why". Pay it off only with what Google itself says: it calls Carbon state-of-the-art in cybersecurity (C1, attributed) and is starting with a subset of cyber defenders before developers, businesses and consumers, with no date (C0, C9). Don't add a motive Google didn't state.
- H9/H10: Carbon isn't open to developers yet, so frame prices as "when it opens up". The $7.76 is AA's figure at standard ($4/$20) pricing.
- H11: name both tools (Claude Code vs Antigravity CLI). This 59 is a different index from the Intelligence Index 59, so label it on screen so the two don't blur.
- H12: the "still the second-lowest of any model AA tested" context is part of the claim. It has to stay in the line, not get cut for time.

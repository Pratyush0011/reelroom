# 05b: Direction for "Google's new AI just ranked #1. You can't use it yet."

## Style
Primary: **D (calm tech-reviewer clarity)**. Secondary: **A (paper-trail documentary)**.
Why this fits: the whole story is a number (59 vs 58) and the fine print around it: which ranking, as of when, and who can actually use the model. Style D's "one clean hero visual per point" turns each beat into a single leaderboard row or one big number. Style A puts the real Google, Artificial Analysis and Vals AI pages on screen at the three trust moments: who gets access, who led on 27 Oct, and who is #1 on Vals.
Not Style C: launch-hype energy fights the story. The script never says "launched", and the point of the reel is that #1 means one point.

## Motion recipe for this reel
- **Target visual change every:** ≤ 1.8 s. The plan has 17 scenes and 11 emphasis punch-ins, which is 28 changes over the voiced ~42.5 s, or about 1.5 s per change. Each `board` also lands its rows about 0.5 s apart.
- **Signature moves (3):**
  1. **The leaderboard row as hero object.** One new `show` set, `board`, carries every rank: big rows, a crown that drops onto #1, and a permanent note tag ("AA Index v5 · as of 2 Nov" / "Vals AI: #2 (about 70% accuracy) · as of 2 Nov"). The dimmed Opus row in beat 4 sits on the world's surface colour with muted text: lower contrast, full opacity, never ghosted.
  2. **Padlock slam.** On "can't", a padlock slams in with a flash and shake (s2): the "not public yet" beat, as a picture.
  3. **Receipt drop.** The real page drifts in tilted, with one phrase highlighted on the spoken word. This happens three times: Google (who gets access), the AA v5 article (Opus led on 27 Oct, right after the surprising claim), and Vals AI (behind Claude Sonnet 5.5).
- **Transitions:** flash on the padlock and on the AA receipt (shutter = "evidence"), glitch into the 58 and into the Vals catch, whip/slide/zoom elsewhere. No transition repeats on adjacent cuts.
- **Emphasis words per scene:** 1, at most 2. Only spoken words that are *not* already big on screen: "Google's", "can't", "small"/"first", "ranking", "But", "behind", "day", "brand-new", "another", "means", "opens".
- **B-roll plan:** none. Brand-heavy topics make stock queries risky (the fetcher skips brands), and every beat here is a number or a document. Brightness comes from the colour worlds instead.
- **Colour worlds:** a different world on every cut (blue → coral → sky → teal → yellow → teal → violet → pink → paper → teal → blue → sky → ink → coral → yellow → violet → sky). `ink` is used once only, for the Vals catch card, with shake.
- **Sound:** whoosh/whip on each cut (engine default), a shutter on the flash cuts, a click on every emphasis word. The padlock slam is a flash plus shake.
- **Hook frame (0–1.5 s):** frame 0 already shows "Ranked #1. Not public yet." with the leaderboard row "#1 Gemini 4 Carbon (high) 59" and the tag "AA Index v5 · as of 2 Nov" under it. In the first 0.5 s the headline words settle in and a gold crown drops onto the row. At about 1.2 s, "Google's" gets a punch-in. The padlock lands on "can't" in the next cut. No Google logo, no Opus row in the hook.
